# Third-party notices

The custom mods derive from Herdr's user-customizable app mods and use its GPUI mod API. The layout records the built-in source version in `cloned_from`. No Herdr application binary is distributed here.

Renderer dependencies are installed from the pinned npm lockfile. Their license metadata and installed license files remain with those packages; they are not vendored in this repository.
